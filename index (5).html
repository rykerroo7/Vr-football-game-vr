GRIDIRONVR

Be every player on the field. Call a play from the playbook, drop back at quarterback and throw to a spot — then switch instantly to the receiver and go get it. Hit the hole at running back, or flip to defense and trigger a tackle as an inside linebacker, safety or outside linebacker. Four downs. First to 21 wins.
